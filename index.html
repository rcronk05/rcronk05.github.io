Prototype Checkweight System

I designed and tested a prototype checkweight system to sort bagged pacifiers due to bagger error. This prototype cost less than $100, in comparison to the $100,000 Mettler Toledo quoted for their checkweight system. It also could eliminate a position of an employee checking the bags, which would save an additional $50,000 a year. I was also able to reduce the number of doubles from 2% to 1%, which, when bagging millions of pacifiers a year, makes quite a big difference.

Arduino/C++ • SolidWorks/3D Printing • Optimization/Problem Solving
